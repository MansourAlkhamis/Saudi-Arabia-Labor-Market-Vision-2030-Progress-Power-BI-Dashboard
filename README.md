# Saudi Arabia Labor Market & Vision 2030 Progress

## Overview
Analysis of Saudi Arabia's labor market transformation from 2017 
to 2024 using official GASTAT data. Covers quarterly unemployment 
trends, female workforce participation, Saudization by sector, 
and regional employment gaps. Built using Power BI.

## Key Findings
- Saudi unemployment dropped from 12.3% in 2017 to 7.8% in 2024 
  — a 37% improvement in 7 years
- Female participation more than doubled from 17.4% to 36.2% — 
  surpassing Vision 2030 target of 30% ahead of schedule in 2021
- COVID caused a sharp spike in Q2 2020 — labor market recovered 
  fully by 2021 showing strong resilience
- Construction and Agriculture remain 95%+ expat-dependent — 
  biggest Saudization challenge remaining
- Government and Finance lead Saudization across all sectors
- Al-Baha and Najran unemployment exceeds 10% vs Riyadh at 5.5% — 
  regional inequality persists
- Vision 2030 transformation is real but uneven

## Tools Used
Power BI, Excel, DAX measures

## Data Source
General Authority for Statistics (GASTAT) — stats.gov.sa
Quarterly Labor Market Publications 2017–2024
